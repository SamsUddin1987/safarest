  home/static/js/cad/interaction/cad-split-view.js  (new)

Installation:
1. Back up your current project.
2. Extract this ZIP into your Django project root, preserving folders.
3. Allow the two existing files to be overwritten and add the new JS file.
4. Hard-refresh the CAD workspace (Ctrl+Shift+R).

Usage:
- Click the small split-screen symbol immediately after MEASURE in the bottom status bar.
- CAD and Spreadsheet will appear together, side by side.
- Drag the vertical divider left or right to change the space shared by each pane.
- The selected split ratio is saved in this browser.
- Click the split symbol again to return to the previously selected single-pane view.
- Clicking the CAD or Spreadsheet tab also exits split mode and opens that tab.
- Manual MEASURE updates do not close split mode.

Notes:
- The CAD and spreadsheet engines remain the existing engines; this change only
  changes their viewport layout.
- Browser interaction testing has not been performed in this environment.
